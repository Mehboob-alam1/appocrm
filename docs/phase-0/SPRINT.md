## Waitlist rule

Count only people who said **yes to a beta ping** on WhatsApp, from your target profession—not friends/family unless they match the persona.

---

## If PIVOT

Change city or profession, reset CSV, rerun **Week 1 Day 4–7** mini-sprint (7 days max) before coding more features.
