# Phase 0 — Validate before building (Weeks 1–2)

Goal: confirm that **solo** business owners in one city + one profession feel real pain tracking clients after phone/WhatsApp calls—and would try a dead-simple app—**before** writing MVP code.

**Master plan:** [../APPOMATRIX_CRM_PLAN.md](../APPOMATRIX_CRM_PLAN.md)

---

## Outcome

By end of Week 2 you should have:

| Deliverable | Target |
|-------------|--------|
| Locked niche + city | One sentence you can repeat (“Independent AC techs in Pune”) |
| Communities joined | 3–5 active WhatsApp or Facebook groups for that profession **in that city or state** |
| Conversations logged | **12–20** short chats (group posts + DMs + 3–5 voice calls if possible) |
| Beta waitlist | **15+** people who said “yes, message me when there’s a test app” |
| Go/no-go decision | Documented in [go-no-go.md](./go-no-go.md) |

---

## Week 1 — Focus + find people

### Day 1–2: Pick niche and city

1. Read [niche-selection.md](./niche-selection.md).
2. Score 2–3 profession options; pick **one** profession + **one** city (or metro + “within 30 km”).
3. Write your **hypothesis** at the top of [validation-log.csv](./validation-log.csv) (see `hypothesis` row in comments—also set in go-no-go doc).

### Day 3–5: Join communities

Use [community-discovery.md](./community-discovery.md) to find groups. Prioritize:

- Groups where **owners/operators** post (not only job listings).
- Recent activity (posts in the last 7 days).
- Local or regional (city name in title helps).

**Rules**

- Lurk 24–48 hours before posting; read group rules.
- No spam, no app links yet—this week is **listening and asking**.
- Introduce yourself honestly (solo builder, learning how they work).

### Day 6–7: First outreach wave

- Post **one** neutral question per group (templates in [outreach-templates.md](./outreach-templates.md)).
- DM 5–10 people who replied helpfully; use [interview-guide.md](./interview-guide.md).
- Log every touch in [validation-log.csv](./validation-log.csv).

---

## Week 2 — Depth + go/no-go

### Day 8–10: Concept test (still no app link required)

- Share the **one-screen pitch** (voice note after call → follow-ups today) in 1–2 groups where admins allow it.
- Ask: “Would you try this on Android if it stayed on your phone only?”
- Run 3–5 **10-minute phone calls** with willing owners; same guide.

### Day 11–12: Waitlist

- Collect names + WhatsApp numbers in a simple sheet (column in CSV: `waitlist_yes`).
- Target **15+** explicit yeses from **strangers**, not friends/family.

### Day 13–14: Go/no-go

- Complete [go-no-go.md](./go-no-go.md).
- If **GO**: note locked niche/city in [../APPOMATRIX_CRM_PLAN.md](../APPOMATRIX_CRM_PLAN.md) (add a “Validation result” subsection) and start Phase 1.
- If **NO-GO**: adjust niche/city or problem framing; run another 1-week micro-sprint—do not start MVP until criteria met.

## What “success” sounds like (qualitative)

You want quotes like:

- “I forget who I quoted until they call again.”
- “WhatsApp is full—I can’t find old jobs.”
- “Notebook gets wet / lost.”
- “I’d use something **after the call** if it’s one tap—not another big app.”

Red flags:

- “We already use NeoDove / TeleCRM for 10 agents.” (wrong persona)
- “I don’t follow up; customers call me.” (may still be users, but weaker pain)
- Polite likes but **zero** DMs and **zero** waitlist signups after 2 weeks (distribution problem—change channel or niche)
