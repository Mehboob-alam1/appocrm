# Finding WhatsApp & Facebook communities (Phase 0)

Goal: **3–5 groups** where your target users actually hang out—not generic “business motivation” groups.

---

## Where to search

### Facebook

1. Facebook search: `"[city] [profession]"` — e.g. `Pune electrician`, `Lagos tutor`, `Jakarta tukang AC`.
2. Filters: **Groups**, sort by **Recent activity**.
3. Look for: member posts asking for advice, sharing rates, complaining about customers—not only admin job ads.
4. Join 5–8; keep 3–5 where you’re approved and activity is real.

### WhatsApp

WhatsApp has no global directory. Common entry points:

- **Facebook group posts** — “Join our WhatsApp group” with admin approval.
- **Trade associations / training institutes** — alumni or technician batches often have WA groups.
- **Local market / industrial area** — shop owners know batch groups (respect privacy; ask for admin intro).
- **YouTube / Instagram** — creators in “[trade] tips” sometimes link WA communities in descriptions (verify legitimacy; avoid scam invite farms).

Avoid:

- Public “group link” aggregators with no clear moderation (spam, unrelated members).
- Groups with 1000+ members and zero discussion.

### Offline (high signal)

- Visit a spare-parts market or coaching hub once; ask 3 owners: “Is there a WhatsApp group for [trade] in this area?”
- One offline intro often beats hours of online search.

---

## Vetting checklist (before you invest a week)

| Signal | Good | Bad |
|--------|------|-----|
| Last post | Within 48 hours | Admin-only, months idle |
| Member posts | Questions, job stories | Only ads and links |
| Group rules | Allow questions | “No promos” with zero discussion |
| Geography | City/state in name or posts | “Worldwide entrepreneurs” |
| Persona | Solo owner / technician | Only HR hiring posts |

---

## Join etiquette

1. **Read rules** — note if surveys or “research” need admin permission.
2. **Profile** — real name + photo; optional one-line bio (“Building tools for small service businesses—learning from experts here”).
3. **Lurk** — 1–2 days; like/comment helpfully on one thread if appropriate.
4. **Ask admin** if unsure: short message that you’re doing **research**, not selling yet.

Admin DM template:

> Hi — I’m researching how [profession] owners in [area] track customer follow-ups after calls (notebook, WhatsApp, apps). Would a short question to the group be okay? No product links. Thanks.

## Search query cheat sheet

Replace `[city]` and `[trade]`:

- Facebook: `[city] [trade] group`, `[trade] technicians [state]`, `[trade] business owners`
- Hindi (example): `[city] इलेक्ट्रीशियन`, `AC technician [city]`
- Tutors: `[city] home tuition`, `[city] coaching centre owners`
- Salons: `[city] salon owners`, `beautician [city]`

Use the language **most posters use** in that city—not necessarily English.
