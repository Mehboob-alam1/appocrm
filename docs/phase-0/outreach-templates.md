# Outreach templates (Phase 0)

Adapt tone to the group. **Week 1:** questions only. **Week 2:** soft concept test. **No Play Store link** until beta.

---

## Post A — Neutral pain discovery (Week 1, safest)

**English**

> Quick question for fellow [profession]s in [city/area]: after customer calls or WhatsApp chats, how do you remember who to call back and who still owes a visit/payment—notebook, WhatsApp pins, memory, or something else? Trying to learn what actually works on busy days.

**Short variant (character-limited groups)**

> How do you track follow-ups after calls—notebook, WhatsApp, or memory? Genuinely trying to learn from [city] [trade]s.

---

## Post B — Follow-up depth (after some trust)

> What’s the last follow-up you **forgot** that cost you money or annoyed a customer? (Happens to everyone—I’m collecting real stories for research.)

---

## Post C — Concept test (Week 2, if rules allow)

> I’m exploring a **very simple Android app** for **one-person** businesses: after a call, you speak a 10-second voice note (“Sharma ji, AC service Friday, ₹800”); phone turns it into text under that contact; home screen shows “who to follow up today.” Data stays **on your phone**—no big CRM dashboard. **Would you try something like that** if it’s free and one-screen? Not selling yet—want 15 people for a later test. Comment or DM if interested.

---

## DM — After someone replies helpfully

> Thanks for sharing—that’s exactly the kind of thing I’m trying to understand. Would you be open to a **10-minute call** this week? I’m not selling anything; just 5 questions about how you work. Or happy to chat here if easier.

---

## DM — Waitlist (Week 2)

> Thanks again. I’m building a small test version for Android—local on phone, voice note after calls, daily follow-up list. Can I save your WhatsApp number to ping you when a **beta** is ready (probably a few weeks)? No spam—one message when it’s ready.

---

## Voice / phone intro (10 seconds)

> Hi, I’m [name]. I’m researching how [trade] owners in [area] track customers—not selling anything today. Is now still okay for about 10 minutes?

---

## What NOT to do

- Drop app store links or “sign up now” in Week 1
- Argue that CRMs are bad—listen first
- Pretend to be a customer if you’re researching (be honest)
- Cross-post identical text to 10 groups same hour (spam filters)

---

## Local language

If the group uses Hindi, Tamil, Bahasa, etc., **translate Post A and C** into that language; keep the same meaning (research → concept → waitlist). Machine translation is fine for a draft; ask a fluent friend to fix idioms before posting in sensitive groups.

Example opening (Hindi, illustrative):

> [शहर] के [पेशे] साथियों से सवाल: कॉल/WhatsApp के बाद फॉलो-अप कैसे याद रखते हैं—कॉपी, WhatsApp, या याददाश्त?
