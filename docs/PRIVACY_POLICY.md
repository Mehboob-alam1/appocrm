# Appomatrix CRM — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026  
**App:** Appomatrix CRM (Android)  
**Contact:** Replace with your support email before Play Store publish.

## Summary

Appomatrix CRM stores your customer contacts, notes, call logs, and follow-up dates **on your device only**. We do not operate a cloud account for the free/beta app.

## Data we store on your phone

- Names and phone numbers you enter or import from your phone book
- Voice notes converted to text on-device (via your phone’s speech recognition)
- Text notes, pipeline status, follow-up dates, and call log timestamps

## Data we do not collect (beta / local-only build)

- No analytics SDK in this build (you may add Play Console vitals only via Google)
- No sale of personal data
- No uploading contacts to Appomatrix servers in the current version

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Contacts | Optional import from phone book |
| Microphone | Voice notes after calls |
| Notifications | Remind you when a follow-up time arrives |
| Internet | Used only to open WhatsApp/call links and optional feedback form in browser |

## Export & delete

- You can export a CSV from **More → Export contacts**
- Deleting a contact removes their notes and call history from the app
- Uninstalling the app removes all local data

## Future paid features

If you enable cloud backup in a future version, that will be optional, encrypted, and described in an updated policy before billing.

## Changes

We will update this policy before any release that sends data off-device.
