# Phase 4 — Early growth (months 3–5)

**After** Play listing is live and Phase 2 feedback is addressed.

## Channels (same niche + city first)

- WhatsApp / Facebook groups from Phase 0
- Trade association newsletters (one paragraph + Play link)
- YouTube “day in the life” shorts with app visible (optional)

## Referral (simple)

- Message: “If you invite another [profession] owner who installs, both get 1 month free **when** paid backup exists”
- Track manually in a sheet until billing ships (Phase 5)

## Product (from feedback)

| Theme | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Language | Hindi (or local) strings — `flutter gen-l10n` when ready |
| Reminders | Morning digest notification (future build) |
| Trust | Short video: “data never leaves phone” |

## Metrics (lightweight)

- Play Console: installs, uninstalls, crashes
- Weekly: active devices (ask beta form “still using?”)
- Target before Phase 5: **200–500** weekly actives in one niche

## Do not

- Buy ads until retention is proven
- Expand to tele-sales / team CRM (NeoDove lane)
- Add dashboards users didn’t ask for
